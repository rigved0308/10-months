My Pigguuu,

Happy ten months, my love. ❤️

Sometimes I think about how one question — “Will you be mine?” — somehow became one of the most important questions I have ever asked. And you saying yes on 22 December 2025 gave me a person, a feeling, and a little world that I never want to take for granted.

I still think about Campal and those first days, about how everything was new and exciting. I think about January, when you had exams and so much on your mind, but still found little ways to make time for me. That meant more than I probably managed to explain.

February gave us the beach, Valentine's Day, and our first kiss — not some perfectly planned movie scene, but our own slightly chaotic, completely real moment. And honestly, I wouldn't trade that realness for a perfect scene. March gave us those days at Adilson, when being close to you felt so natural: the comfort, the trust, and the little moments when the rest of the world seemed far away.

Then came the distance again. Missing you isn't always dramatic; sometimes it is just wishing you were here for one ordinary thing, wanting to tell you something while looking at your face, or wanting a hug that a screen simply cannot give. Even in the quiet chapters, you have stayed important to me.

I remember May, too — especially when I had that little accident and you took care of me. You gave me reasons to smile when I needed them. You saw my childish side, my vulnerable side, and all those bits of me that aren't always easy to explain. Thank you for making me feel cared for instead of making me feel like I had to be strong all the time.

And August… how could I forget? You arriving on time, renting the scooter yourself, giving me my first bouquet, my first Hot Wheels, the T-shirt and that jar full of little things. I still hold those gestures close. Then I got to make the room special for you, with the card, the rose petals, the umbrella surprise and the cake. Seeing you happy made every bit of planning worth it. And you made me a birthday card too — another small thing that means a lot because it came from you.

September gave us matching outfits, the beach, that late-night drive, your first waffle, and so many little moments of care. The memories aren't precious because every second was perfect. They're precious because they were ours.

Thank you for loving me in your way, for showing up, for letting me know you, and for giving me memories I can revisit on days when I miss you too much. I know we won't always have easy days. I don't expect us to be perfect. I just hope we keep choosing honesty, patience, kindness, and each other — even when distance makes everything harder.

Ten months down, and I still want the simple things with you: more silly conversations, more hugs that last too long, more food dates, more new firsts, more days where we do absolutely nothing special and somehow that becomes my favourite day. I want to keep learning you, keep making you smile, and keep building something that feels safe and real for both of us.

If I could go back to that day and ask, “Will you be mine?” all over again, I would still hope for the same yes. And if I could teleport anywhere right now, I wouldn't choose somewhere fancy. I'd choose wherever you are, just to hold you a little longer.

Happy ten months, my Pigguuu. I love you — not just for the big memories, but for all the little moments that make you you.

Yours, always trying to make you smile,
Riggu ♡
